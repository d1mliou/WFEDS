RULES, and the first four are where this task goes wrong if you are careless.

1. Judge the whole answer, not sentences in isolation. The six questions are about the
   record. One wrong statement anywhere makes question 1 or 2 true for the record.

2. A claim may rest on the OPERATOR'S REQUEST as well as on the payload. Each record
   prints the request verbatim. Do not call something unsupported merely because the
   payload does not repeat it, when the request states it plainly and the payload does
   not contradict it. A start time the operator supplied is the standard example.

3. Advisory sentences and limitation statements are NOT factual claims about the
   payload. They never make question 1 or 2 true on their own. They are judged by
   questions 5 and 6. Qualitative statements about settlements becoming harder to
   evacuate are not road claims; `cut_off` counts settlements, never roads, and only a
   claim that attaches a number, an identity or a specific state to a ROAD is a road
   claim.

4. Coverage is about what a reader is told, not about wording. An hour may be named
   directly or covered by a range the answer states accurately. Numbers need not be
   repeated. But a transition moved to the wrong hour, attached to the wrong entity, or
   reported in the wrong state is NOT covered, however fluent the sentence.

Also:

- Ground truth is this record's payload and this record's request. Not your own
  knowledge of wildfires, not what the pipeline could in principle compute.
- Judge the answer as written. Do not repair it, and do not soften a verdict because
  the rest of the answer is good.
- Use only the six questions and the values the schema permits. Do not invent a seventh
  criterion and do not import one from any other evaluation.
- The record carries TWO separate lists, required critical events and required
  disclosures. Question 3 is answered on the first and question 4 on the second. Do not
  mix them: an absent disclosure never fails question 3, and an absent transition never
  fails question 4.
- Every failure needs a verbatim quotation from the answer plus the payload field, row
  or period, or the event_id or element_id, that establishes it. A failure you cannot
  quote is not a failure.
- If a record has no narrative surface, set judgeable to false, leave the six
  questions null, and say why in one sentence. Do not guess what the answer would have
  said.
